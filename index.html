What is GhostSlot?

GhostSlot is a free campus app that shows which classrooms, library pods, cafés and sports courts are free right now, and books one for you. It runs in any phone or laptop browser.

How it works

  1. Say what you need. Type it the way you'd say it, like "quiet place near CSE for 90 minutes with charging", or tap the filters.
  2. See the closest free match. Free spaces are ranked by fit and by walking time from where you are. Set your spot by GPS, by picking a block, or by scanning the GhostSlot QR on a door.
  3. Lock it in. Book the room in one tap. A timer runs at the top, and the room frees itself when your time is up.

Who it's for

  • Students find a study room, library pod, café table or court, and book it.
  • Faculty see their classes and find a free room for an extra lecture.
  • Facilities staff spot rooms left powered on while empty, and print door QR codes.

Where the data comes from

Classrooms and labs use the timetable plus live bookings. Libraries, cafés and courts use campus Wi-Fi crowd levels, which are simulated in this prototype until the campus feed is connected. One demo room is sensed live by two ESP32 boards reading Wi-Fi signals.
